The false binary

Seawater cooling in India is proposed, not deployed

Andhra Pradesh's policy floats it for Tarluvada, but the environmental clearance names no water source at all. The real fight today is groundwater vs. municipal vs. treated non-potable vs. recycled blowdown — and almost nobody discloses which. Seawater can cut freshwater demand, but moving the problem from an aquifer to the ocean is not a closed loop.

The verification gap

Once a clearance is granted, nobody checks

A groundwater NOC fixes an extraction cap. Nothing requires publishing what is actually drawn or discharged, and no agency verifies the declared source against on-site practice. The EU mandates disclosure; India does not. Without independent verification, "water-neutral" is unfalsifiable — which is the definition of greenwashing.

Why software

Heat availability tracks an unpredictable IT load

Oracle's 2018 patent claims a fixed hardware topology on a steady seawater loop. India's fleet is mixed air- and liquid-cooled, inland, and fragmented. A forecasting and dispatch layer is what makes any treatment hardware usable — an RO skid, an ETP, a BWSSB plant, a future MD unit. That layer is the product.

STAGE 03 · RECOVER

Membrane distillation module

The core hardware, simulated at the pore. Hot concentrated blowdown flows across a hydrophobic membrane; only water vapour crosses. Fluoride, nitrate and dissolved salts are non-volatile — they physically cannot follow.

Flux model

Antoine driving force

Vapour flux is J = B·(Pw(Tfm)·aw − Pw(Tpm)), with saturation pressure from the Antoine equation. Flux rises exponentially with feed temperature — which is exactly why grade-matched waste heat, not electricity, is the right input, and why RO could never use this energy at all.

Temperature polarisation

boundary layer

The membrane surface is never as hot as the bulk feed. Boundary layers eat part of the driving force — the polarisation coefficient τ is what separates the ΔT you measure from the ΔT the membrane actually sees. Ignoring it is how paper designs overpromise flux by 30%.
